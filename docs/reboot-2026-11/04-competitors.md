# Competitor assessment

**Every price and feature below was checked on 11 November 2026.**

## Verdict

RushRank has real competitors now. Six small products target the individual fraternity chapter, and three of them (GreekPad, RushBoard, Rush+) overlap heavily with RushRank's feature list. The incumbents (Phired Up's ChapterBuilder, MyVote and CampusDirector; OmegaFi's OmegaRecruit) sell through headquarters and campus councils and are built for sorority formal recruitment or year-round lead tracking. They are indirect competition.

- **Price anchor:** chapter tools cluster at about **$80 to $220 per semester, flat for the whole chapter**.
- **Where RushRank is ahead:** nobody advertises a chair-driven session where the whole room sees one PNM at a time, combined with round cutoffs, a bid board with outcome tracking, duplicate merge and a PowerPoint export.
- **Where RushRank is behind:** texting PNMs, native apps, and any visible track record.

## How this was researched, and its limits

- Each claim comes from a page fetched on 11 Nov 2026, with the URL beside it.
- Feature claims are almost all the vendors' own marketing. Independent reviews barely exist beyond App Store ratings.
- "Not seen" means the feature was not mentioned on the pages fetched. It does not mean the product lacks it.
- **Reddit could not be fetched**, and search returned no r/Frat or r/Sororities threads. There is no forum evidence here of what chapters actually use.
- Two vendor sites (GreekPad, RushBoard) render with JavaScript. Their copy and prices were read from the sites' own script bundles, so the rendered page may present them differently.

## Direct competitors: tools for one chapter

### GreekPad

- **URL:** https://greekpad.com/
- **Targets:** individual fraternity chapters; the site's keywords also mention sororities.
- **Features:** PNM tracking with an intake link; brother notes that exec can lock; bid voting with Bid / Waitlist / No Bid, exec-only controls and an audit trail; a big-screen "Presentation Mode" with a live tally; QR scan-in; automatic no-bid below a threshold; scheduled SMS reminders and no-bid texts from a chapter number; one-click bid list export. Access is by member passcode and exec passcode.
- **Pricing:** $14.99 per month; or "$79.00 every 6 months"; or "$149.90 annually · 2 months free". Includes "Unlimited PNMs + chapter members" and "10 free SMS credits / month". Founder copy also says "Five bucks to build your dashboard, refundable for 14 days"; how that relates to the subscription is unclear.
  - Source: https://greekpad.com/ (site bundle and structured data), checked 11 Nov 2026.
- **Reviews:** none found.

### RushBoard

- **URL:** https://rushboard.net/ (vendor not named)
- **Targets:** individual fraternity chapters.
- **Features:** live roster; QR check-in for rushees; every brother rates and comments from his phone in real time; a drag-and-drop Bid / Waitlist / Reject board synced to every phone; a bid-day call tracker; custom tags; join links. The offered way to clean up duplicate check-ins is deleting a profile; no merge was seen.
- **Pricing:** not published. Source checked: https://rushboard.net/, 11 Nov 2026.
- **Reviews:** none found.

### Rush+

- **URL:** https://rushpl.us/ (Blanket LLC)
- **Targets:** fraternity and sorority chapters.
- **Features:** prospect profiles showing who has talked to whom; interest forms that feed a pipeline; events, tasks, chapter chat and "Rush Groups"; voting rounds to make cuts or select prospects; recruitment targets against bid-day headcount; stage-conversion analytics; an agent that builds a profile from an Instagram handle; iOS and Android apps; data export.
- **Pricing:** free trial tier (10 seats, 25 PNMs, 1 interest form, unlimited voting rounds); Lite at **$120 per semester** (25 seats, 75 PNMs); Full Access at **$220 per semester** (unlimited). "Flat rate for your whole chapter — not per person." A yearly option says "Save up to 27%"; yearly prices are not published.
  - Source: https://rushpl.us/pricing, checked 11 Nov 2026.
- **Reviews:** App Store 5.0 from 16 ratings (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rush/id6745244081).
- **Caution:** the homepage stat counters rendered as "0+", and the vendor's blog claims "2,000+ chapters" with no source.

### Greek Connect

- **URL:** https://www.getgreekconnect.com/ (Greek Connect LLC)
- **Targets:** whole-chapter management for fraternities, with sorority pages; councils and headquarters by quote.
- **Recruitment features:** PNM pipeline from first contact to bid; rushee profiles with photo, major, hometown and Instagram; comments, values-based tags and officer-defined rating fields; group rating and voting on bids; rush events with RSVP and check-in; an accepted PNM becomes a member on the same roster. Sources: https://www.getgreekconnect.com/fraternity-management-software and https://www.getgreekconnect.com/story/fraternity-rush-app
- **Not seen:** rush intake forms, CSV import or export, rush analytics, a bid list builder.
- **Also includes:** chat, attendance, points, dues, polls, meal plans, guest lists; iOS, Android and web.
- **Pricing:** free 14-day trial, "then $120 per semester" at the lowest member band shown. The price scales with chapter size; the other bands were not visible. Councils and headquarters: "Contact for pricing".
  - Source: https://www.getgreekconnect.com/pricing, checked 11 Nov 2026.
- **Reviews:** App Store 4.6 from 95 ratings (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1576086717).

### PNMPulse

- **URL:** https://pnmpulse.com/ (Nankervis Digital LLC)
- **Targets:** fraternities, sororities and other student organizations; an enterprise tier for councils.
- **Features:** a PNM self-service portal with headshot, resume and calendar; QR check-in; custom forms with deadlines; bulk email; deliberation tools (discussion queues, real-time sentiment polling, instant voting); public and private notes; cycle-over-cycle comparison; CSV and Excel export.   - Source: https://pnmpulse.com/, checked 11 Nov 2026.
  - A third-party listing disagrees: "Starts at $400/year" (https://sourceforge.net/software/product/PNMPulse, checked 11 Nov 2026).

## Where RushRank can stand out

1. **The room, not the roster.** Every direct competitor has a PNM list and some form of voting. None advertises what RushRank already does: a chair advances one PNM at a time, every phone shows the same face, the chair can lock, and the count ticks up live. GreekPad's presentation mode is the nearest. This is the demo, and it is the reason to switch from a spreadsheet.
2. **The whole decision trail.** Rounds, cuts with a preview, a bid board, then offered / accepted / declined against a cap, with an audit log. Competitors mostly stop at Bid / Waitlist / No Bid. Only RushBoard tracks bid-day calls.
3. **Clean data.** Nobody advertises duplicate merge, and only ChapterBuilder advertises import. Chapters collecting names from Instagram, a form and a spreadsheet will have duplicates on day two.
4. **Artifacts.** A PowerPoint deck and PNM cards for the bid meeting. No competitor checked advertises either.
5. **Coverage before the vote.** "Who has actually met him" is in Rush+ and RushRank only.

RushRank is also free today, because no billing exists. That is a difference only if it is a deliberate choice; see item 7 below.

## What that implies we build

In priority order. Each maps to an item in `03-features.md`.

1. **Make the live session honest and complete,** because it is the differentiator. Anonymous voting has to work or come out. Add who has not voted yet, a timer that locks, and a big-screen view for the projector. GreekPad already sells the big-screen view.
2. **Text PNMs.** Four competitors do; RushRank does not. An event reminder to a filtered list is the smallest useful version. Email through the existing MailerLite integration can ship first, with SMS behind the same interface.
3. **Let PNMs check themselves in** with an event QR. GreekPad, RushBoard and PNMPulse have it. Today a brother must scan each person.
4. **Finish the bid end:** a cap that can be set, a call tracker for bid day, and outcome in the export.
5. **Keep the exec role real.** GreekPad separates member and exec access cleanly. RushRank's exec role cannot currently be assigned.
6. **Install to the home screen.** A web app manifest and offline check-in close most of the gap with the native apps for far less work than building one.
7. **Decide on price before the next chapter asks.** The market has set an expectation of roughly $80 to $220 a semester. Free is a valid answer if it is deliberate.

## What not to chase

- **A whole-chapter platform** (dues, points, chat). Greek Connect bundles rush into one at the same price and has 95 App Store ratings. Competing there means building their product.
- **Council and headquarters features.** CampusDirector and ICS own formal recruitment through institutional relationships.
- **Profile-building from Instagram handles.** Rush+ and RushPilot market it. It raises consent questions about people who never agreed to be in a database, and it is not why a chapter would choose RushRank.
